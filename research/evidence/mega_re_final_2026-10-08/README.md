## Confidence rule

The imported MegaRE terminology is preserved:

- `VERIFIED` means the stated local operand/call/store/type/flow relation is established at its written scope; it does **not** imply complete function semantics.
- `STRONG_INFERENCE` is a supported interpretation with an explicit ceiling.
- `UNKNOWN` is an intentionally unjoined edge, not permission to fill the gap from naming or analogy.
- A request, pointer, clear, lock, numeric equality or matching address does not by itself prove success, ownership, current generation, quiescence, final use or runtime cadence.
