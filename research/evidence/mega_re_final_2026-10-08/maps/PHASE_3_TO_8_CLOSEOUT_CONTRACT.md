Each material pipeline, root or seam records these as separate facts or explicit unknowns:

1. Acquisition/root identity and provenance, not complete subsystem scheduling or algorithm.
2. Initialization, static conditional use/order and reset/shutdown placement, not measured runtime selection/frequency/order.

## Requirement verdicts and dependency exceptions

For every phase-specific requirement use **MET**, **BOUNDED**, or **NOT_MET**, and record the reviewed scope, positive evidence, first missing edge, queue ID/reopen trigger and downstream consequence.

- **MET:** the positive result required at that scope exists; it does not certify unexamined fields, slots, modes or runtime behavior.
- **BOUNDED:** identify the actual searched source boundary or unavailable evidence. Explicitly classify it as blocking or non-blocking for the proposed successor dependency. BOUNDED is not automatically satisfied.
- **NOT_MET:** the required result is missing or contradicted.

NOT_MET and blocking BOUNDED requirements prevent a normal transition. Non-blocking exceptions require edge-specific reasons; one blanket waiver cannot replace a required positive portfolio. **Unvisited is not bounded.** A root/interface missing from an edge claimed as connected is a blocker, not a prose exception. An unresolved dependency cannot be ignored while a downstream claim assumes its unproved side.

Use the phase-specific positive minima. A disconnected symbol inventory, all-negative ownership portfolio, report title, validator pass or arbitrary metric threshold cannot satisfy them. Record selected viable work and reasons for deferring other high-value live sources rather than silently narrowing a phase.

## Closeout package

At an actual phase review, create a report using `templates/PHASE_CLOSEOUT_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`; do not prefill research deliverables simply to make paths exist. The package must include:

- human scope, current phase and proposed successor, distinguishing plan/governance approval from transition/research approval;
- phase-specific requirement verdicts and a positive portfolio, separately crediting reused results and new reductions;
- all-current-root/family visibility where required by the phase, including newly discovered families and unserved seeds;
- exact first missing edges, original source/reopen limits, blocking/non-blocking/input-runtime dispositions and the successor's dependency consequences;
- red-team/correction/provenance review of newly foundational and high-impact claims, with actual primary samples and alternatives tested;
- quantitative coverage by build and by outcome type, without equating coarse classification, naming, confidence and semantic mechanism understanding;
- current maps/reports linked to claims/ledgers, a dedicated checkpoint, one exact next action, and actual validation results;
- an explicit decision: remain in phase with a live bounded source, pause for evidence/authorization, or perform an authorized transition. A coherent narrative is not completion.

Before and after closeout, run both validators and progress reporting. Run relevant isolated regressions after control/helper/validator changes. STATUS, loop, checkpoint receipt, selected queue row and latest journal must agree; bounded/resolved histories and exact reopen triggers must survive. Structural validators do not adjudicate semantic sufficiency or grant authority. Review counters change only through their recorded research/strategic rules; a governance-only repair preserves last research outcome and counters.

A transition receipt is GOVERNANCE and names the reviewed package before changing phase. A READY frontier uses a matching live `OPEN_PHASEn` row; parked governance/authorization states use their actual non-READY disposition, not a fabricated research source or completion sentinel.

Alternatively, a **human-reviewed constrained-stop decision** must record the actual measured budget/input limit, unavailable evidence, remaining viable static sources, reasons useful work cannot continue now, and exact future reopening conditions. Missing runtime traces, an exhausted local method or a polished narrative are not whole-corpus exhaustion. With useful static evidence/budget remaining, continue high-information Phase 6/7 work rather than treating Phase 8 as an escape hatch.

Constraint-driven final outputs retain all `06_DELIVERABLES.md` categories, explicitly label incomplete earlier milestones and avoid a fictional all-phases-complete assertion.
