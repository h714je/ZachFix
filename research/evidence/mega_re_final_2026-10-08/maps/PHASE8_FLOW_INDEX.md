## How to read confidence and numbers

`VERIFIED` is selected primary-established operand/call/store/type/flow evidence, not complete function semantics. `STRONG_INFERENCE` is supported interpretation at its stated scope. `HYPOTHESIS` is plausible but unproved. `DISPROVEN` preserves an exact retired stronger interpretation. `UNKNOWN` marks an intentionally unjoined edge.

Addresses and `+offset`/stride/mask operands are hexadecimal. Hex byte sizes have `0x`; record counts, distances, rates and screen dimensions are decimal unless explicitly prefixed. Function receiver/input/output/caller/callee tables and source anchors accompany the diagrams. API requests, pointer access, local clears, stable addresses and matching IDs must not be read as success, ownership, completed destruction, same generation or complete producer populations.
